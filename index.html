FAQ

Things people ask before donating.

Is donating blood safe for me?
100% yes. Every donation uses a new sterile needle, takes under 15 minutes, and your body replaces the volume within 24 hours. You can safely donate every 90 days.
How do you verify donors are real?
Every donor uploads a college ID and government ID. We cross-check with the institution and require a one-time health screening before activation.
What happens after I submit an emergency request?
Within seconds, every compatible verified donor inside your radius gets a push notification + SMS. Available donors hit "Accept" and a direct chat with you opens immediately.
Is there any cost?
Zero. The platform is fully free for both donors and requesters. We're a campus initiative — not a business.
Can I choose not to be contacted at night?
Yes. Set your availability hours in your profile. Critical/life-threatening requests can override only if you opt in.
Who can become a donor?
Any verified student aged 18–65 in good health, weighing above 50kg, who hasn't donated in the last 90 days.
